## 0. AMENDMENTS — product-owner resolutions (2026-08-11, `PDR-0014`)

The plan was reviewed by four `axiom-planning` lenses (reality / architecture / quality / systems)
and synthesised to **CHANGES_REQUESTED** with three blocking issues. Reality found **zero
hallucinations across ~45 checked claims**; the sequencing, decomposition and pinning-test
discipline reviewed clean. The three blockers are resolved here. **These resolutions override the
task text below where they conflict.**

**B1 — take D5; it is not optional.** Stamping and comparing `primary_level` was left as an
undecided "Decision required" while §4 claims the deliverable is *"the provenance guardrail is no
longer breached"*. It is not: an `L0_5_dual_resource` checkpoint resumes into
`L1_full_observability` with **eight of nine identity fields colliding** and every guard green.
**Resolution: implement it in this batch.** It is ~2 lines in files three tasks already have open,
and `PDR-0012` (strict no-tech-debt until 1.0) removes the deferral option — a batch whose headline
claim is false at the moment it lands is debt by definition.

**B2 — the dead-agent filter is in scope, and it is WIDER than the review found.** Verified
independently: `substrate_mask` is an *action-id range* mask (`actions < custom_action_start_id`),
with no relation to `dones`. So `interact_mask = (actions == INTERACT) & substrate_mask` carries no
dones filter, and the cost debit at `action_executor.py:149` (`env.meters[interact_mask] -=
interaction_costs`) sits **upstream of the instant/multi-tick split**. Consequence: dead agents are
charged interaction costs on **both** paths — the `active_mask=torch.logical_not(env.dones)` guard
on the multi-tick path guards *progress* but not the *debit*. **Resolution: filed as its own
pre-freeze unit** (see tracker) rather than folded into task 3, because it is now a distinct defect
in a different function from the one task 3 rewrites. The dangling §5 "*decide with D2/D5 at task
0*" cross-reference is void — the owner is the new ticket.

**B3 — SETTLED BY CONFIG, and it resolves the opposite way from "delete the clamp".** The review
gated this on measuring whether effects overshoot `1.0`. They do, routinely, and config alone
proves it: bars ship `initial: 1.0` with `bounds.max: 1.0`, while `SLEEP` applies
`energy = energy + 0.8` (computes **1.8**) and `EAT` applies `satiation + 0.4` (computes **1.4**).
`torch.clamp` is currently the **only** thing re-ceilinging them.

Two corrections follow:
1. **There are FOUR meter-clamp sites, not six** — `affordance_engine.py:209`, `:310`,
   `action_executor.py:60`, `:150`. The fifth grep hit (`world/expression/functions.py:404`) is a
   `smoothstep` maths helper and is unrelated to meter bounds. Any "all six move together"
   instruction is over-counted.
2. **Deleting the clamp at `:209` alone is not a cleanup — it removes the only enforcement of a
   declared bound.** `bars.*.bounds.max` is INERT (declared, validated, drives nothing), so
   deleting the clamp would make `bounds.max: 1.0` a *lie at runtime*. Under `PDR-0007` that is the
   worst failure mode a declarative product has.

**Resolution: do not delete the clamp, and do not keep it as a hardcoded constant either. Wire
`bars.*.bounds` and make all four sites read the declared bounds.** This converts a magic constant
into the config value it was always standing in for. It is a scope increase to WS-1, accepted
deliberately: `PDR-0012` forecloses deferral, `PDR-0007` forecloses documenting-the-limitation, and
the fix moves **Config-surface coverage** rather than merely repaying debt. Per `PDR-0007`'s
definition-of-done it needs a config-in/behaviour-out test: author a pack with `bounds.max: 0.5`,
apply SLEEP, assert energy settles at `0.5` and not `1.0`.

**Discounted, on policy.** Systems proposed retaining `torch.clamp` as a silent safety net. Rejected
— a silent re-floor is the *"silent fallbacks hide breaking changes that should fail loudly"*
antipattern in `CLAUDE.md`, reinforced by `PDR-0012`/`PDR-0013`. The observation is kept; the remedy
is not. Bounds semantics must be explicit across all four sites, or fail loudly.

**Warnings W1–W5 stand as written by the synthesis** and are folded into their tasks: W1 declare
tasks 6+7 one atomic merge unit; W2 pin the `primary_level` membership check to run *before*
`_cache_artifact_path`; W3 have task 3's precondition call the public `can_afford` rather than
reimplement it, plus a `meters == cost` boundary case; W4 resolve `config_hash_warning` in this
batch rather than retaining it "for DemoRunner parity" (retention on parity grounds is the
*"keeping obsolete code just in case"* antipattern); W5 state determinism/device assumptions behind
