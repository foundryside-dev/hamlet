# PDR-0067 — the grant's `Last reviewed` stamp is corrected to 2026-08-16, at an approved touch

Date: 2026-08-16   Status: **accepted** (owner-approved — a `vision.md` touch, escalated and
approved at the second 2026-08-16 `/own-product` resume)
Author: Claude (standing product owner)
Owner sign-off: **yes** — the resume's grant question offered "Confirmed, and correct the stamp"
as the recommended option and the owner chose it.

Related: `PDR-0050` (the same correction, 2026-08-15, and the rule it set: the stamp moves only
at an approved touch), `PDR-0038` (the pattern — factual corrections to `vision.md` are offered
at the grant re-confirmation and applied on approval)
Evidence: `docs/product/vision.md` grant block and amendment-log entry dated 2026-08-16

## Context

`current-state.md` carried, from the first 2026-08-16 resume: *"vision.md's authority grant reads
`Last reviewed: 2026-08-15`; you re-confirmed it unchanged on 2026-08-16. Not touched … Offer
stands for the next resume."* At the second resume of the day the owner confirmed the grant
unchanged and approved the correction in the same answer.

## The call

Correct the stamp to `2026-08-16`, restate the re-confirmation history in the Status line, and add
the amendment-log entry. **Scope unchanged** — same autonomous list, same escalation taxonomy,
`PDR-0046` still governs the push. This is a factual correction, not a vision change, exactly as
`PDR-0050` classified it.

## Rationale

The rule from the 2026-08-15 amendment holds: the agent does not touch `vision.md` on its own
initiative, even to fix a date it knows is stale; it carries the debt and offers the fix at the
next grant re-confirmation. That is what happened, and it cost one resume of a stale stamp — the
price of never having the agent edit the vision file unasked.

## Reversal trigger

- If the stamp is found stale at two consecutive resumes without an offer being made, the
  "offer at re-confirmation" rule is not being followed and the resume script needs the offer
  baked in, not remembered.
