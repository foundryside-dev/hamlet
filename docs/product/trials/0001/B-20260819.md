## Search pre-registration (Appendix A.2 — surfaces to try, in order, written BEFORE authoring)

1. **`gridnd` at 5-D** (`stratum.yaml`: `type: gridnd`, `dimension_sizes: [4,4,4,4,4]`,
   `topology: hypercube`) — the B2 landscape; the proven Trial 001 shape at 6-D, one dimension
   down. Expect this leg to just work.
2. **Organism-as-population (the corpus's own predicted workaround, tried deliberately, not as
   a fallback):** K agents ARE the organism's occupied cells. Root-at-point-A via spawn
   position; "growth outward" as agents stepping to adjacent cells (gridnd movement is
   unit-step per dimension, which IS adjacency); organism coherence via a `group`-scoped VFS
   variable and/or DAC shaping penalizing dispersion. This is where the entity-as-set-of-cells
   stress lands: I pre-register the expectation that **no surface makes a cell durably
   "occupied" after the agent that visited it moves on** — a creep trail — other than possibly
   VTC occupancy claims (next line).
3. **VTC occupancy claims** (`vfs/vtc.py` vocabulary: occupancy claims exist as a compiled
   transition concept) — try to declare claimed-cell persistence as the "organism footprint".
   If unreachable from YAML or agent-bound rather than durable, that is the entity-as-cell-set
   facet failing and gets classified there.
4. **Food warehouse as affordance + items**: a static food source as an affordance at a
   position (B1/B2) with effects transferring "biomass"/energy into a group- or agent-scoped
   meter on interaction.
5. **DAC (`drive.yaml`)**: approach/proximity shaping toward the food source and extrinsic on
   biomass gain — "must LEARN to spread toward food" is a reward-surface claim; leg-(c)'s
   reward assertion probes it directly.
6. **B3 (contested, diagnostic)**: two organisms = two `group`s of agents contesting one food
   source; expect group scope to carry per-organism identity; contention itself proven
   already by O/H-adjacent machinery (no clearing step needed here — first-come suffices).
7. **Per-cell world state, direct form** (a variable indexed by cell, written by the world):
   scopes are {global, agent, agent_private, item, pair, group, affordance, zone, message} —
   there is no per-cell scope. I pre-register the expectation this direct form is **ABSENT**
   and the trial's honest question is whether surfaces 2+3 reach the Spec's meaning anyway.
