## Remaining finding — R2a, High: last saved binding cannot validate all historical rows

**Contract:** old canonical events/fingerprints and POP seen-event records remain verbatim across a fresh row permutation and subsequent genuine native boundaries. Native integrity validation must accept valid history while rejecting wrong event-to-world identity.

**Concrete witness:** complete batch1 in binding `(A,B)` with canonical event `(batch1, agent_idx=0, world_id=A)`. Restore fresh into `(B,A)`, complete batch2, then save. The amendment says the new episode's destination binding becomes the snapshot's saved binding `(B,A)` and also requires validating **every historical event's** `agent_idx -> exact world ID/sequence` correlation against **that saved binding**. Batch1's unchanged valid event now maps agent0 to B under that rule and is rejected. Rewriting it to B or changing its index/fingerprint would violate the other explicit requirement. The same contradiction can affect a retired world's retained older last_completed_content.

**Finite remedy:** replace the universal last-binding rule with per-boundary historical provenance. Validate the snapshot's last accepted/closed token and its events against its saved binding; validate each retained older canonical event/dedup/completion record against the immutable accepted binding for that record's own batch/episode. Freeze one bounded native representation for this: either the existing canonical record already carries a sufficient exact accepted binding/identity under its owner receipt, or retain an ID-keyed/boundary-keyed binding receipt only for boundaries referenced by retained records. State which owner persists and validates it (POP/PW05/T25), reject missing/contradictory provenance, and keep old event bytes/fingerprints unchanged. No unbounded full historical journal is required, but current saved binding cannot stand in for older bindings.

**Required witness:** batch1 `(A,B)` -> fresh restore `(B,A)` -> genuine batch2 -> save -> fresh restore again; unchanged batch1 content and any retained A/B prior-completion markers validate against their own original binding, batch2 validates against `(B,A)`, next receipt follows requested destination order.
