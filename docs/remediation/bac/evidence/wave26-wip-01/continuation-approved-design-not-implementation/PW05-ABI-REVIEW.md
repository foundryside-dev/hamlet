## Material corrections

1. **Completion replacement has an unaccounted live-state gap.** The private recipe snapshots native state, reconstructs through a fresh factory, and installs the reconstructed owner. Actual WS `prepare_restore` reconstructs a backend and loads only its `checkpoint_state`; published RND/adaptive native formats contain no parameter gradients. Thus native equality alone does not establish preservation of all live state promised by this proposal (which explicitly retains gradients), even though parameters, Adam, RMS, queues and RNG are represented. Specify exactly which live values completion may change and how the off-side candidate preserves other applicable state, including gradients, or obtain a sourced proof that gradients are absent at this boundary. Add a nonempty-gradient/nonempty-tail completion preservation control; do not infer it from native snapshot equality. Do not silently relax the all-private-state requirement. This is a design gap, not a claim that an uninspected provider currently leaves gradients populated.

2. **Prepared-completion lifecycle and expected-refusal equality need an exact rule.** WS snapshot advances owner revision, even on failure; call also advances revision. The proposal takes live snapshots while preparing, yet calls preparation pure, promises stale-token checking before mutation, and forbids observations between preparation/install. Specify a single pending completion slot, equal-content prepare replay, changed preparation refusal, allowed/blocked bank operations while prepared, and the complete bank revision update points (including diagnostic snapshots). Reject an existing pending completion before taking any new live snapshot. Explicitly distinguish unchanged learning/RNG/cursors from owner revision invalidation on a failed preparation; if full revision equality is required, the current recipe cannot claim it. Check all bank/token/content/revision preconditions before the first install, with unexpected installation failure quarantining the entire bank. A malformed last outcome or candidate failure must leave prior learned state, dispositions, receipt cache and service cursors unchanged. Provide the shared off-side recipe just as concretely: obtain an actual detached callable backend without ambient random reconstruction, invoke its actual completion algorithm in accepted lane order, then prepare/install the final native candidate. An opaque provider prepared token is not itself a callable backend.

3. **Temporary memory is acknowledged but not estimated or admitted.** The required preallocation estimate lists steady tensors and unknown overhead; “report separately” does not define the completion allocation report or when the consumer can refuse it. Private completion currently retains snapshots plus temporary owners, post-completion snapshots and destination prepared candidates for potentially every participant. WS itself clones constructor outputs and restore candidates, so peak copies exceed one steady backend per world. Specify a conservative known tensor-copy peak formula and lifetime schedule, participant multiplier, separate unknown workspace/container overhead, and a synchronous report/refusal point before completion allocation as well as eager construction. A sequential preparation strategy may retain all final candidates, but must account for those retained candidates plus the current world's temporary copies. No fallback, capacity reduction, skipped update or population cap is acceptable. The model arithmetic itself is correct: RND 5P plus six Adam steps; ICM 4P plus twelve, with float-profile assumptions and lazy moments labeled.

4. **Finish the public records and initial/empty lifecycle boundary.** The design names `PreparedBankCompletion`, `WorldServiceReceipt`, `AllocationEstimate`, and snapshot metadata but leaves several exact field/type/ownership domains implicit. Freeze their finite schema, particularly the completion token versus its public `prepared_dispositions` receipt, lane-ordered dispositions, row versus shared aggregate service deltas, selected device/dtype, and initial snapshot null accepted/binding/cache fields. INITIAL must have no fabricated episode-zero EventId; accepted identity capability references must not be serialized or deep-copied as authenticated tokens. Specify how the bank supplies truthful zero-participation evidence required by the public ENV empty-abandon path, or name that missing producer handoff explicitly. An empty accepted attempt cannot remain an uncloseable bank episode or fabricate a completion. At activation use the now-source-verified literal kind `activation` and exact payload `{accepted: actual_token}`; completion receipt kinds/fields must be the exact public ENV contract.

5. **Resolve all-inactive shared service and provenance semantics explicitly.** The proposal simultaneously promises no inactive service effects and calls a real shared grouped provider at vector_tick. The public local-service contract permits an empty shared call at a newer tick to service queued work. State whether an all-inactive bank batch makes no provider call (consistent with the brief's inactive contract), and add a shared pending-tail negative control; an empty call cannot be treated as automatically mutation-free. For shared nonempty batches retain grouped admission, pacing and overflow. WS stamps have no vector_tick field: record the reserved tick and full preparation identity in bank-owned pending metadata, correlate every reward/service context with it, and never claim the stamp itself carries tick provenance. Define equal inactive preparation/replay without accidentally replaying a prior active event or allocating a new opportunity.

Current native diagnostics support owned model/Adam/RMS/count/LRU/queue/history/RNG evidence, with receipt/cache metadata owned separately. Snapshot reads are revision-sensitive and cannot masquerade as harmless observations of a pending restore. EPS/Count absent complete pure validators remain a PW06 dependency; no fake validators or old-format restoration are authorized.
