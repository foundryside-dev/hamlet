## Material findings

1. **Bind every authoritative check/attempt to its actual source and custody.** The proposed attempt row currently supplies a round, path and free-form result, while candidate identity is only global or attached to component receipts. Require a hash-bound authoritative check record (or equivalent inline schema) containing its candidate manifest/tree, command/selection, timestamps, exit/counts or explicit unrun reason, environment/hardware, controller/worker/child identities, import/thread/debug custody, raw logs and verifier identity. Validate required fields and their referenced bytes. Historical/diagnostic attempts must not inherit the terminal candidate accidentally. Allow explicit unavailable custody for retained failures, but prevent those records from satisfying an executed/qualified witness requirement.

2. **Make independent review custody machine-checkable.** A review role/path/hash preserves text but does not bind the reviewer, independence or reviewed candidate/receipt. Require reviewer identity, author/producer identity or an explicit independently evidenced separation, reviewed subject identity/hash and bounded disposition/scope. Validate subjects against the exact candidate/check being supported. A terminal review must not silently stand in for a component review or vice versa. Retain rejected/superseded reviews as historical evidence when selected.

3. **Seal directory membership and reconcile semantic coverage.** Null-hash attempt directories need an explicit descendant path/size/hash inventory in the final plan and assembly-time exact membership recheck; checking existing file hashes alone misses added attempts/logs after sealing. Also recheck the finite top-level interval set. Enforce unique (source, check, attempt) identities, terminal pointers belonging to their check, and declared attempt status. Reconcile the full selected interval to indexed attempts or explicit non-attempt artifact roles. Byte preservation alone cannot establish that every attempt is semantically accounted for. Reject uncovered/multiply conflicting attempt records; permit shared log objects through explicit aliases.

4. **Make child and dependency closure explicit.** Generic dependency role/required_by strings do not establish which named children each executed check requires. Require each check's expected child witnesses and verifier/helper dependencies, with source-bound witness references and status. A missing required executed witness fails final validation; an explicitly unrun child remains unrun and cannot satisfy that gate. Do not derive portable dependencies by blindly following legacy absolute paths inside raw receipts.

5. **Specify publication-candidate and plan-integrity boundaries.** Assembly output remains a review candidate until root supplies approved navigation pre/post hashes and independent source-equivalence/publication guards. A proposed manifest must not claim publication or qualification. Assembly must validate the plan schema/status and copy/reference/alias consistency, hash-bound receipt and predecessor/selection-plan relationships, normalized rooted paths and absence of symlinks in every parent component, and exact sizes/hashes immediately before use. Check current-chain prior hash before proposing an append and preserve all existing entries. If final navigation bytes are not supplied, label their output an incomplete proposal rather than a sealable delta. Partial/existing output must fail closed and never be reused as successful assembly.
