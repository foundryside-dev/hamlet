## Meaning of child success then outer failure

The root-adopted, still-unimplemented core design gives a replay `device_invocation` its own immutable `completion_failed() == False` when that replay invocation's actual completion phase succeeded. A later parent outer-union failure does not rewrite that child result; the outer boolean names the parent phase and identifies no failing device or owner. POP source proves parent GPU-capable tensor/Parameter effects can continue after child return: Q/target forward, hidden/display Q, backward/gradient/optimizer, target copy, current/successor tensor work, and later parent result construction. These are parent-owned effects whose healthy state may become uncertifiable on an outer completion failure. CPU assignments to ledgers, counters, episode progress, metrics and pointers may already have published by then; they are part of the parent's overall consistency/fail-closed decision, not evidence that replay's earlier completed GPU work failed.

Replay-owned storage/priority/local-generator state is reached through the listed child calls. A successful child completion certifies its own declared device work at that cut under its producer contract. The parent source alone does **not** show replay-owned asynchronous work after that cut. It can still hold returned batch tensors, pass parent/ENV views into replay, call replay again later, and expose `self.replay_buffer` as a raw alias. Whether a later parent CUDA kernel touches replay-owned storage through a returned batch/input alias, or whether a subsequent replay call has uncertain work, requires the replay supplier's exact alias/copy and invocation inventory. Merely seeing a replay device in the outer union, common poison, or a child False cannot justify quarantining every replay owner. Conversely, a child True or actually established replay-owned alias/effect uncertainty needs its own producer/parent response before replay lock release. The root-adopted design explicitly leaves this attribution to parent integration; no per-device receipt or blanket all-owner policy is inferred.
