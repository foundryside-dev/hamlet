## Why preissuing release is not selected yet

An alternative is issuing release before BANK preparation. It avoids a between-seal-and-install ENV call, but authentic saved bytes alone do not prove the joint boundary has actually validated the child candidates/independent cadence/ledger. The current world contract treats restored-boundary issuance as healthy effectful authority, not a provisional unvalidated handle. Neither the current BANK source nor the ENV supplier proves early issuance lawful under that semantic attestation. It can also leave an installable world release after later child qualification fails.

Therefore do not preissue merely to bypass callback admission. Independent ENV/outer-source review may prove an existing narrow prepared-release meaning and failure revocation that makes it legal; until then the recommended ordering prepares actual candidates first and issues release separately afterward. An exact existing release may be checked as data, but it never replaces current prepared-record validation at all later callbacks.
