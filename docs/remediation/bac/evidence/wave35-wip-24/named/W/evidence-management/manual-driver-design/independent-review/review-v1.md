## Why this addition is necessary and finite

The fixtures do more than ask for an unrelated physical step beside a trained BANK. Conversion close embeds actual ENV step counts/meters into the same accepted episode's canonical content, obtains the learned BANK's prepared/committed dispositions, and produces a real completed ENV record used by destination restore. Restore Correlation explicitly compares original accepted identity, batch, roster, world sequences, completion content, participant IDs and per-world cursors against that ENV record. A separate compiled BANK cannot provide those same-owner dispositions. Current POP's only full path also adds compiled curiosity/replay/learner work, while its prepare/claim prefix cannot finish. The supplied absence of a manual physical boundary therefore matters to the preserved joint assertions.

Between-vector retirement is justified by preserving the actual four-both/one-A schedule and physical-count-backed completion content. There is no separate literal `assert B_count == 4` in these fixture bodies; the count follows the specified physical schedule and is consumed in completion. This distinction avoids inventing an extra historical assertion, while retaining the actual scenario required by the task and requirements capsule. A-only synthetic events alone would not justify retirement, and sparse BANK ticks do not justify arbitrary physical-clock overrides. The design states both limits.

The proposal does not need a new production population/outcome importer. Existing generic receipts authenticate explicitly local cooperative role ownership; their scope never claims Q/replay/curriculum effects absent from the fixture. Keeping these owners, canonical local summaries and existing ENV/BANK close checks is a smaller valid composition than manufacturing POP authority or introducing another completion subsystem.

## Contract assessment

- **Single effects and independent geometry:** actual compiled predecessor/actions/activity remain ENV-owned. One session call completes the physical kernel and publishes its owned result; no public unfinished POP decision escapes. It performs zero curiosity stamp/reward/service or Q/replay/optimizer work. The separate original width3/action2 BANK event remains exactly once. The shared reward factoring retains the same physical DAC/temporal/terminal path with declared zero intrinsic input; its ordinary integrated equivalence must be tested, not inferred from the new method name.
- **Participation and clocks:** explicit initial retirement uses real intent/ready/commit records before activation, not forced dones. Mid-episode retirement acts only between completed vectors, leaves counts/tick unchanged and cancels terminal effects once. ENV physical lifetime ticks remain sequential; BANK sparse, all-false, B-only and cached-event controls keep their separately supplied currentness rules. This preserves five/private or nine/shared admissions and the adaptive/drop/offset scenarios without hidden physical-derived service.
- **Authenticity and restore:** the session is ENV-issued and tied to exact population-role binding, lifetime/current accepted identity and activation/participation. Refusal tests must cover copied/foreign/stale/expired authority and conflicting ordinary runtime binding. Fresh-bound binding must not perturb the existing restore phase assertion. Reset/restore invalidate per-episode authority; restored last-closed history cannot step or close a receiver's live episode. No live simulator restore API is introduced to accommodate BANK live-mode controls.
- **Completion and failures:** real canonical local content, prepared BANK dispositions, ENV freeze, BANK commit, ENV close and nine-role release remain mandatory; the existing repeated BANK commit identity check survives. Physical return/receipt is not a core RNG completion certificate. Failure after entering physical effects must retain the exact exception and stage, deny another healthy action, and use the source-owned runtime_failure path; cleanup failure is a note. Pre-effect refusal cannot manufacture success. Synthetic BANK failure uses its actual owner policy, never a blanket unrelated-owner poison or replay of an earned event.
- **Custody and scope:** current C2 domain-before-ENV exclusion, callback/reentry fences and genuine C1 direct BANK composition remain the contract. None must not be nested under an active parent, and delegated runtime scope is not direct BANK authority. Full source-complete device/RNG inventory and authentic sealed-outcome consumer remain explicitly required at the later shared C2 integration gate.
