## 3. Persistent support admission, actual-request weights and atomic updates

**One beta-independent persistent invariant:** factory/domain/storage/update/restore admission validates closed schema, finite reducer/configuration state, canonical identities and **positive representable probability support**. For every changed/restored nonempty domain, call public PS `sample_units` on its complete staged float64 vector with recorded alpha/law, **beta=0, batch_size=1**, using a discarded clone of the owned/prepared Generator. This is a support probe only. Its synthetic beta, receipt and scratch draws are neither installed nor persisted; recorded beta/tick and every caller/source/live RNG remain unchanged. Empty domains require no probe. Factory admission has no domain to probe until it materializes one. Stage **all** affected domains and the running maximum and install only after every support/identity/reduction check succeeds. No private PS API, duplicate score arithmetic or new sampling mode is needed.

This invariant follows actual PS: `_prepare` validates positive finite priorities, centered scores and full-population normalized probabilities, but skips final-weight preflight at beta0 and successive B=N; `_receipt` returns all-one weights for those cases. `_final_weights` exponentiates beta-scaled log weights directly. A persistent state is therefore not rejected because an untempered reciprocal or hypothetical beta1/B1 weight overflows. In particular, `[1e-6,1e308]` has tiny **positive representable** support at alpha1; it is not a support failure.

`sample_batch` alone checks **actual requested B and recorded effective beta** through public PS on its staged Generator. Preserve positions/order/q/population/mode/weight rule and float64 weights, without normalization or a preliminary untempered reciprocal. Replace only the public RC objective label with window/anchor_transition; stable unit_ids[B] separately map positions to handles. Conditional q is proposal evidence, not marginal inclusion probability. Successive B=N retains all-one weights; beta0 and fractional-beta acceptance follow PS exactly. This does not promise every B can be served at every beta.

Reuse/refactor SB's existing selected-window packing inside SB with explicit `(episode,start,end)` selections and the prepared receipt. Preserve real prefix lengths, actual successor masks at every real row, terminal handling, component presence/None optionality and detached transport. The learner applies the unit weight once after the real-row window mean or anchor loss, then batch-means windows. No timestep reweighting or padded-row denominator; existing uniform/legacy laws and receipts remain unchanged.

`update_priorities(unit_ids: Tensor, td_errors: Tensor, *, loss_mask: Tensor) -> None` requires nonempty int64[B] known active handles, real floating [B,W] TD errors and bool[B,W] loss masks on the TD device, with W covering each actual context. Detach errors, ignore unselected/padded entries and require selected real errors finite. A malformed/retired ID, wrong shape/mask, nonfinite/nonrepresentable reduction or genuine support failure refuses atomically; finite current-beta/B1 **weight** overflow alone does not reject the update. This storage operation claims no optimizer rollback.

Atomic sample: stage domain/handles and a cloned owned Generator, run actual-B/current-beta PS and construct/validate the complete owned batch before no-fail ownership transfer. Any probability/weight/packing/allocation failure leaves domains, handles, priorities, max, caller/source/owned RNG and clocks unchanged. A failed first sample does not admit its domain.
