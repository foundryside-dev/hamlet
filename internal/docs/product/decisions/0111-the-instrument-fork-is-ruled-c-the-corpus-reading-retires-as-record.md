## Context

Criterion 3 required two blind re-runs to reproduce their verdicts. O reproduced (the cheap
one); B did not — §7's reject branch fired on classification disagreement (`PDR-0106`), and
the commissioned `tensorNd` audit then established the disagreement as **genuine search
variance**, not executor error (`PDR-0110`, Branch A). The instrument's own acceptance
criterion rejected it, honestly. Three branches were escalated: (a) amend the protocol with a
search-variance control and re-run criterion 3; (b) accept publishing headline verdicts only;
(c) retire the reading as record.

## Options

- **(a) Amend + re-run** — most rigorous; costs another blind re-run cycle plus the G-P1
  protocol-defect treatment; publishes a rate eventually, under an amended instrument.
- **(b) Accept with caveat** — publishes headline-only sooner; the caveat is real (both B
  re-runs agreed FAIL/FAIL) but the instrument would carry a known-failed criterion.
- **(c) Retire as record** — nothing from this corpus publishes as a rate; the nine trials
  keep their full evidentiary value as records; the north-star returns to `UNREAD` pending a
  redesigned instrument.

## The call

**(c). The corpus reading is retired as record.** Consequences, all mechanical from here:

1. **No number from this corpus ever publishes as a rate** — not the 6-of-9, not the split,
   not the ≥80% arithmetic. The north-star row's status changes from "unpublishable pending
   ruling" to **RETIRED AS RECORD**, terminally for this corpus.
2. **`PDR-0095`'s fired trigger is discharged by retirement** — the condition it protected
   (no publication over a rejected instrument) is now permanent for this corpus, so the
   trigger has nothing left to guard.
3. **The records keep their value.** Six trial records, two blind re-runs, the comparison
   documents, the audit, and the 30+ by-catch tickets stand as the actual product signal —
   which axes fail and why. `PDR-0078`'s framing survives: the low reading was a finding
   about the substrate, and the finding is banked even though the rate is not.
4. **D, E, J remain runnable as record, not reading** — optional, unchanged from `PDR-0110`.
5. **The north-star metric itself returns to `UNREAD`.** Reading it again requires a
   redesigned instrument — at minimum a search-variance control (pre-registered surface
   checklist) of the kind branch (a) described, over a revised corpus (candidates Q/R and
   the statistician's substrate-naive stratum are already captured). That is a **future bet
   for the owner to promote**, not residual scope of this one.
6. **The bet itself is substantially complete.** Its deliverable was the instrument
   (`PDR-0077`); the instrument was built, used for six trials, turned on itself, and
   rejected by its own criterion — the acceptance-on-the-instrument design worked exactly as
   intended. Remaining scope is record-keeping (optional D/E/J, retro-derivations, the
   2026-10-06 pack-disposition clock).

## Rationale

The instrument's honesty is worth more than its number. Branch (b) would ship a rate from an
instrument whose acceptance criterion fired; branch (a) is real work that competes with the
token migration the owner has already prioritized (`PDR-0108`). Retiring cleanly preserves
every finding, closes the escalation, and leaves instrument redesign as a properly-shaped
future bet instead of an open wound blocking the scoreboard.

## Reversal trigger

Reopen if:

- the owner commissions a successor instrument and rules that the retired corpus's records
  should be re-scored under it (re-scoring is then a NEW reading under the new instrument,
  never a revival of this one);
- any workspace or public artifact quotes a rate, denominator, or split from this corpus as
  a published reading — that is a breach of this PDR, to be corrected on sight;
- the ruling itself is revisited by the owner (their call to make at any resume).
